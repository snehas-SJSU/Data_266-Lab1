# Task 3 — Results: CycleGAN Monet ↔ Photo Style Transfer

**Author:** Ritika Mukesh Neema

## Architecture & justification
- Two ResNet-based generators (`G_A2B`: monet→photo, `G_B2A`: photo→monet,
  the Kaggle-scored direction), each 9 residual blocks + 2 downsampling /
  2 upsampling conv layers, instance normalization, reflection padding
  (standard Zhu et al. 2017 design — avoids checkerboard artifacts vs.
  transposed-conv-only designs, and instance norm suits per-image style
  transfer better than batch norm here since batch_size=1 is standard for
  CycleGAN).
- Two 70×70 PatchGAN discriminators (`D_A`, `D_B`) — patch-level real/fake
  classification rather than whole-image, which is what makes CycleGAN's
  texture/style transfer sharp instead of blurry.
- Losses: LSGAN adversarial loss (MSE, more stable gradients than vanilla
  GAN's BCE), cycle-consistency L1 (λ=10, the core constraint that lets
  training work *without* paired data), identity loss (λ=5, preserves color
  composition — keeps a photo passed through `G_B2A` from needlessly
  recoloring content that's already photo-realistic).
- 50-image replay buffer (image pool) for discriminator updates — the
  original paper's stabilization trick against oscillation from only ever
  seeing the newest generator output.
- Linear LR decay to 0 over the second half of training (`n_epochs_decay`),
  standard CycleGAN schedule.

## Cycle-consistency verification
`evaluate_local.py` cycles every sampled image through both generators
(A→B→A and B→A→B) and reports the resulting L1 reconstruction distance —
low values with the same shape/composition on visual inspection of
`outputs/pred_A2B/` and `outputs/pred_B2A/` is the check that the
constraint is actually doing its job (not just minimizing the number on
paper).

## Notes / limitations
- Epoch length is defined as the *smaller* domain (Monet paintings, ~300
  images) rather than the larger one (photos, ~7000) — see the comment in
  `src/dataset.py::UnpairedImageDataset.__len__` for why: this keeps each
  epoch's wall-clock time practical for a time-boxed GPU Lab session while
  still exposing the model to the full photo domain over the course of
  training (via per-item random sampling), not just a truncated subset of it.
